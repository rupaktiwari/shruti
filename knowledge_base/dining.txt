Himalaya House dining.

The restaurant, Lakeside Kitchen, is open 07:00 to 21:30. Breakfast is 07:00 to 10:00. Lunch is 12:00 to 15:00. Dinner is 18:00 to 21:30. The kitchen closes at 21:00 for new orders.

Breakfast for Deluxe and Suite guests is included and is a set menu: eggs or a Nepali breakfast plate, fruit, tea or coffee. Standard guests can buy breakfast for NPR 800 per person.

The menu has dal bhat, momo, Newari khaja, grilled trout from Phewa, and a short vegetarian list. There is no room-service menu after 21:00. Water bottles in the room are NPR 100 if the seal is broken.

The bar serves local beer, raksi by the glass on request, and coffee. Alcohol service stops at 22:00.

Outside food is allowed in rooms but not at the pool. The resort can note a food allergy at the front desk. The chat cannot change a kitchen order that was already sent.
