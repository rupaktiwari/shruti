Q: Why is my KYC not verified yet?
A: KYC verification typically takes 24-48 hours. Common reasons for delay
include a blurry ID photo, a name mismatch between your ID and account,
or an expired citizenship card. Re-upload a clear photo under Settings > KYC
if it has been more than 48 hours.

Q: What documents are needed for KYC?
A: A valid citizenship card or passport, and a clear selfie for face matching.
