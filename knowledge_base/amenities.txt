Himalaya House amenities.

The pool is open from 07:00 to 19:00. Towels are at the pool desk. Children must be with an adult.

Wi-Fi name is Himalaya-Guest. The password is given at check-in and is also printed on the key card. It covers rooms and the lobby.

The spa offers a 60-minute Nepali massage for NPR 3,500 and a 90-minute massage for NPR 4,800. Book the spa at the front desk, not through this chat. Last spa booking is 17:00.

Yoga on the lawn is at 06:30 every morning and is free for in-house guests.

Airport pickup from Pokhara International Airport is NPR 1,200 each way for Standard rooms. Deluxe and Suite stays include one pickup. Tell the front desk your flight number at least 3 hours before landing.

Parking is free for one car. There is no EV charger.

A small library and board games are in the lobby. The resort has a kettle and tea in every room. Iron and hair dryer are available from housekeeping.
