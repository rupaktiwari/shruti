Himalaya House is on Lakeside road, Pokhara, about 8 minutes on foot from Phewa Lake and Hallanchowk. The street address used by taxis is Himalaya House, Lakeside, Pokhara 33700.

Pokhara International Airport is about 25 minutes by car. Tourist bus park is about 15 minutes by taxi, usually NPR 400 to 500.

Day trips the front desk can arrange, prices per car not per person: Peace Pagoda and boating NPR 3,000, Sarangkot sunrise NPR 3,500, Begnas Lake NPR 6,000. These are separate from the room rate and are paid to the driver.

The nearest clinic is about 10 minutes by taxi. For an emergency, call the front desk first, extension 9, or Nepal emergency 102.

The resort is not in Kathmandu and cannot book flights. Staff can suggest a taxi or a domestic flight time, but ticketing is outside this concierge.
