Himalaya House rooms in Pokhara.

Standard room. Garden view, queen bed, fan and air conditioning, private bathroom. Nightly price NPR 6,500. Breakfast is not included. Maximum 2 guests. Eight rooms.

Deluxe room. Partial lake view, king or twin beds, air conditioning, private balcony. Nightly price NPR 9,800. Breakfast for two is included. Maximum 3 guests. Five rooms. This is the deluxe rate to quote when a guest asks the price of a deluxe stay.

Suite. Full Phewa Lake view, separate sitting room, bathtub, private balcony. Nightly price NPR 16,500. Breakfast for two and one airport pickup are included. Maximum 4 guests. Two suites.

Whether a room is available is not in these notes. Availability is the live count of free rooms for the requested dates. A quoted price does not mean the dates are free, and a free room is still charged at the nightly price above.
