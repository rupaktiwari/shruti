Himalaya House room rates. Prices are nightly, in Nepali rupees, before the 13 percent VAT.

The price of a standard room is NPR 6,500 per night. Breakfast is not included. A standard room holds 2 guests. There are 8 standard rooms.

The price of a deluxe room is NPR 9,800 per night. Breakfast for two is included. A deluxe room holds 3 guests. There are 5 deluxe rooms. A deluxe stay costs 9,800 rupees times the number of nights.

The price of a suite is NPR 16,500 per night. Breakfast for two and one airport pickup are included. A suite holds 4 guests. There are 2 suites.

How to price a stay: nights are check-out minus check-in. A stay from 21 November to 26 November is 5 nights. Five nights in a deluxe room cost NPR 49,000 before tax (5 times 9,800). Five nights in a standard room cost NPR 32,500 before tax. Five nights in a suite cost NPR 82,500 before tax. Add 13 percent VAT at checkout. The chat can quote this price. It does not take payment.

An extra bed is NPR 1,500 per night. A child under 6 stays free in the existing beds. Late checkout until 14:00 is NPR 2,000 when the room is free.
