Himalaya House guest policies.

Check-in is from 14:00. Check-out is by 11:00. Early check-in and late check-out depend on availability and are not guaranteed. Late check-out until 14:00 is NPR 2,000 when a room is free.

A reservation needs the guest's full name, email, check-in date, check-out date, number of guests, and room type: standard, deluxe, or suite.

Cancellation is free until 48 hours before check-in. Inside 48 hours, the first night is charged. No-shows are charged the first night.

The resort does not take cash deposits in chat. Payment is collected at the front desk on arrival by card or Nepali cash. A booking made in chat is a confirmed hold, not a prepaid stay.

Quiet hours are 22:00 to 06:00. Smoking is only on balconies. Pets are not allowed. The resort cannot store passports overnight.

Lost property is kept for 30 days at the front desk. Billing disputes, refunds, overcharges, safety incidents, and requests for a manager are handled by a human colleague, not by the concierge chat.
